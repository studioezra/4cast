From Data to Decision Advantage

4Cast Decision Intelligence Platform

4Cast is an advanced Decision Intelligence (DI) platform designed to empower organizations with critical insights and recommendations.

Utilizing AI to model and simulate scenarios, 4Cast platform allows decision-makers to explore various options and select the best courses of action to achieve their organizational goals.

Architecture and Trust

SAP
SAP Solution Extension (SOLEX), connecting SAP force structure, personnel, equipment, and logistics data through the 4Cast Data Gateway to support readiness assessment, simulation, and course-of-action comparison.
Deployment
On-premises or private cloud, sovereign cloud and air-gapped deployments subject to customer requirements and technical validation.
Security
SOC 2 Type II attestation. Deployment-specific security controls and customer accreditation requirements are addressed with the customer and delivery partners.
Explainability
Recommendations grounded in explicit decision models with visibility into the data, assumptions, constraints, and drivers behind assessed outcomes.
Human in the loop
4Cast evaluates options and their impact; commanders retain decision authority.
