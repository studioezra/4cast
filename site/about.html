Our Vision

To Empower People to Make Better Decisions

Our mission is to transform decision-making with AI-powered Decision Intelligence, enabling leaders to make smarter, faster, and more impactful decisions at every level – strategic, operational, and tactical – by providing the tools, insights, and recommendations they need.

4Cast Journey

From Vision to Reality Decision-Support Tools

Our journey began with a team of experts dedicated to creating innovative decision support solutions. Recognizing the limitations of existing tools, we developed a proprietary solution tailored to the unique demands of military and defense operations, laying the groundwork for our Decision Intelligence platform.

  1. 2018

    4Cast is Born

    In 2018, we took a leap forward, founding 4Cast with a clear mission: to transform decision-making. Our goal was to empower leaders to turn complex challenges into opportunities by providing the tools and insights needed for impactful decisions.

  2. 2020

    Leading in Defense Innovation

    We refined our solutions to meet the evolving needs of the defense sector. Our commitment to innovation positioned us as a leader in decision support tools for military readiness and beyond.

  3. 2021

    Pioneering AI-Powered Decision Intelligence Platform

    In 2021, we reached a major milestone, integrating advanced neural networks to create 4Cast’s groundbreaking Decision Intelligence platform, supporting decision-making at every level—strategic, operational, and tactical.

  4. 2022

    Expanding into Emergency Management

    Recognizing the need for stronger decision-making across sectors, we expanded into emergency management. Our expertise in readiness and preparedness now empowers emergency organizations to respond effectively to crises, safeguarding communities and ensuring continuity.

  5. 2023

    Strategic Partnership with SAP

    In January 2023, our journey took another giant leap as we partnered with SAP. This collaboration strengthens our capabilities and broadens our reach, delivering AI-powered decision intelligence solutions to organizations worldwide.

  6. 2024

    Building Organizational Resilience

    Leveraging our expertise in military readiness and emergency management, we’ve built a strong foundation for organizational resilience. 4Cast’s platform enables businesses to anticipate challenges, adapt to changes, and connect actions to outcomes. With 4Cast, organizations don’t just wait for the future—they shape it.
