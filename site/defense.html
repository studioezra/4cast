Decision Intelligence

Defense solution families

Build the force tomorrow's missions require.

Connect future missions, capability requirements, force structure and resource constraints to evaluate alternative force designs and investment priorities.

  • What force do we need?
  • Which capabilities matter most?
  • Where should limited resources be invested?
  • How will today's campaigns affect tomorrow's force?

Build the right force for the mission.

Move beyond static readiness percentages by evaluating personnel, equipment, training, availability and sustainment against actual mission requirements. Evaluate force packages and understand which combinations best satisfy mission objectives and constraints.

  • Which units are mission-ready?
  • When will they be ready?
  • What are the critical readiness gaps?
  • Which force package best meets mission requirements?

Getting to the fight is part of the fight.

Evaluate the full deployment chain across road, rail, ports and supporting infrastructure to understand how mobility constraints affect force projection and mission execution. Identify bottlenecks, evaluate alternatives and assess whether forces can arrive where they are needed, when they are needed.

Don’t Just Ask Whether the Force Can Move. Ask Whether It Can Arrive Ready to Operate.

  • Can the force move through the available network?
  • Where are the critical bottlenecks?
  • Which alternative movement plan best supports the mission?

Keep the force operating at mission tempo.

4Cast connects operational demand with logistics, personnel, equipment, maintenance, mobility and critical infrastructure to help leaders understand whether – and for how long – an operation can be sustained.

Know What the Mission Will Need – Before the Mission Creates the Demand.

  • Demand-Driven Logistics: translate mission plans and OPTEMPO into resource demand.
  • Critical Infrastructure: understand how national infrastructure enables or constrains the mission.
  • Operational Endurance: identify emerging resource, equipment, personnel or infrastructure constraints.
  • Adaptation & Regeneration: reassess allocation and force generation as conditions change.
