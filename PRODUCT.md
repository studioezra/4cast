## What 4Cast is

4Cast is a Decision Intelligence platform. It gives leaders options and shows the impact of each one before they commit. Positioning against the category: BI shows what happened, predictive AI estimates what might happen, and 4Cast answers what to do ("Compare Options. Understand Impact. Adapt with Confidence.").

The human decides. 4Cast evaluates options and their impact, and decision-makers keep the authority ("The Commander Decides" / "Human in the loop").

## Audience

All sectors get equal weight on the homepage: Defense & National Security, Utilities & Critical Infrastructure, Government & Emergency Management, and enterprise sectors (supply chain, healthcare staffing, and others). Visitors are senior decision-makers and their planning teams who already have data and dashboards but lack clear options, quantified impact and trade-off visibility.
